See the Face of Christ, Be the Face of Christ

Every home visit, every drive, every gift is an encounter — with our neighbors, and with Christ in them. There's a place for you in this work, whatever time or gifts you have to offer.

"You will find that charity is a heavy burden to carry, heavier than the kettle of soup and the full basket. But you will keep your gentleness and your smile. It is not enough to give soup and bread. This the rich can do. You are the servant of the poor, always smiling and good-humored. They are your masters, terribly sensitive and exacting masters."

— St. Vincent de Paul
Ways to Serve

Home Visits

Vincentians visit families in their homes, listen to their struggles, and offer both practical help and a compassionate presence. This is the heart of our ministry.

Warehouse & Distribution

Sort, organize, and prepare donated furniture and household goods at the SVdP warehouse so they're ready when a family needs them.

Drives & Collections

Help plan and run our seasonal parish drives — 40 Days for 40 Beds, the Blanket Drive, and others — from setup to promotion to collection day.

Food Pantry

Sort food, restock the shopping line, unload trucks, help with facility upkeep, and answer phones at either of our two pantry locations — 30th Street or Boulevard Place.

What to Expect
1
No experience necessary. Every Vincentian started as a beginner — training and an experienced partner come with every home visit at first.
2
Give what time you have. Some roles are a couple of hours a month; others, like home visits, ask for a regular ongoing commitment. We'll help you find the right fit.
3
Formation matters to us. Vincentians take part in ongoing spiritual formation alongside the practical work — this is a ministry as much as it is service.

Ready to Serve?

Reach out and a Vincentian will follow up with next steps — no commitment required to just ask questions.
